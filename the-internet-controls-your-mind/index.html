More very simple claims

The Internet Controls your Mind

(I) If you consume opinions all day, some of them are going to get in your brain. [0]

(II) Verifying opinions helps you control what stays in your brain. [1]

(III) If you consume opinions all day, and rarely verify them, then there will be opinions in your brain that you don’t want.

(IV) The average American spends 6 hours online. [2]

(V) Americans only verify 2% of the opinions they consume. [3]

Conclusion: The internet controls what’s in your brain.
